Bird Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bird Dash handles information when you use the Bird Dash mobile game.

Information We Collect

Bird Dash does not collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, location, or other personal information.

Bird Dash does not use Firebase, third-party analytics services, or advertising services to collect information about players.

Purchases

Bird Dash may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Payment information is processed by Google Play. Bird Dash does not receive or store your credit card, debit card, bank account, or other payment credentials.

How Information Is Used

Because Bird Dash does not collect personal information, we do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or sale.

Information Sharing

Bird Dash does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Security

Bird Dash does not maintain a database of players' personal information. Transactions made through Google Play are handled according to Google's security and privacy practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Bird Dash does not retain personal information collected from players. Because the game does not maintain player accounts or a database of personal information, there is no personal account data stored by Bird Dash that requires a separate deletion request.

Children's Privacy

Bird Dash does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. If the game's target audience or data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bird Dash's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Bird Dash is published by its developer through Google Play. For privacy questions or concerns, users may contact the developer through the developer contact information provided on the Bird Dash Google Play listing.

Summary

Bird Dash does not collect personal information, does not use advertising or third-party analytics, and does not sell or share personal information. Optional purchases are processed through Google Play.
